How this works

  1. Slice — the source is cut into thin horizontal strips (strip height).
  2. Displace — each strip shifts horizontally by noise(y) × amount plus a diagonal depth gradient so displacement ramps across the frame instead of jittering randomly.
  3. RGB split — the R, G and B channels are drawn at slightly different offsets per strip, giving the muted colour fringing.
  4. Layer — 1–4 passes are rendered at increasing scales (1→2×) and blended, producing the woven interference texture.

Each seed is one “experiment.” Change parameters + seed to explore the space, then Download PNG. All processing is client-side — nothing is uploaded.
